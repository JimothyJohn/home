privacy policy

advin.io is a boutique AI research lab in Dallas, TX, operated as a sole proprietorship. This policy covers the advin.io website and communications advin.io sends, including SMS text messages.

what we collect

The website collects no personal information, sets no tracking cookies, and runs no third-party analytics. If you communicate with advin.io by SMS or email, we receive your phone number or email address and the content of your messages, and use them only to respond to you and operate the service you requested.

sms and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are not shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message content and phone numbers are used solely to deliver the service; they are never sold or rented to anyone.

sms consent and opt-out

SMS conversations are user-initiated: you opt in by texting an advin.io number first. Message frequency varies with your use. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

retention and security

Messages are retained only as long as needed to operate the service and are then deleted. Data in transit is encrypted (TLS).
