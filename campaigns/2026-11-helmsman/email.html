three new things in helmsman

Helmsman is the soft PLC you program by describing the machine. This week it learned to show the whole program in 3D, read a panel drawing, and talk to a Universal Robots arm. Each one is shown working below.

HELMSMAN helmsman.advin.io
see it for yourself The playground runs in your browser,
with nothing to install.
01
THE WHOLE PROGRAM IN 3D

Stand the live diagram up off the page and fly into it. Each block's logic sits on the level below, and every scan lights its path as it runs.

Double-click a block to dive in, Escape to climb out. The flat view stays the default, one toggle away.

Recorded live from the playground.

02
PANEL DRAWINGS, READ

Hand Helmsman a panel layout and it knows what's in the cabinet. Every part in the drawing is checked against the device library.

ASCII DXF in. Each part comes back matched, ambiguous or unknown, with nested blocks placed through the ones around them.

The reader's real output on Helmsman's test drawing, animated.

03
UNIVERSAL ROBOTS, CONNECTED

A UR arm's state, straight into your program, read right the first time.

UR e-Series over Modbus TCP. A joint's angle lives in two registers; Helmsman joins them, where reading one alone is off by a full turn. A Structured Text example comes with it.

Register values captured from Universal Robots' simulator, animated.
