# advin.io

The site and brand of advin.io LLC. `BRANDING.md` is the owner's rules for the site; the full brand guide is the `advin-brand` skill (`.claude/skills/advin-brand/`).

- Anything that looks or reads like advin.io (pages, email, posts, decks): load `advin-brand` first.
- Announcing a project's new features, a newsletter, or feature GIFs: load `feature-campaign`. Past campaigns live in `campaigns/`.
- Never send an email or post without Nick's explicit go-ahead.
