advin.io · SMS program description

The advin.io SMS assistant is a conversational text-message service operated by advin.io, a sole proprietorship in Dallas, TX. Authorized users text the assistant questions about advin.io systems — service status, weather observations, schedules — and receive a single informational reply per inquiry.

how to opt in

Opting in is user-initiated: send a text message to +12562977714. Sending a message is your consent to receive the reply to that message. The assistant never sends the first message and never initiates a conversation.

program disclosures

  • Message frequency varies with your use of the service.
  • Message and data rates may apply, per your carrier plan.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out; reply HELP for help, or email nick@advin.io.
  • Mobile information and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
